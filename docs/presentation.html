03 The problem

Most hospital work still runs on paper, phone calls and memory.

Shift changes are spoken, not written

What the night nurse needs to know depends on a five-minute conversation at 8 am.

Desks don't talk to each other

Pharmacy, lab, kitchen and billing each keep their own register. The bill is rebuilt by hand at discharge.

Insurance holds up discharge

TPA approvals are chased over email, and patients wait hours after the doctor has discharged them.

No one can say who changed what

NABH and the DPDP Act expect a trail of every action on a patient's record.

SHDS replaces each of these with one shared record. Every person does their part on it, and the next person picks it up from there.

06 Front door

Patients check themselves in at the lobby kiosk.

  • Mobile number on a number pad, then a one-time code. The kiosk holds a 10-minute ticket for that patient only.
  • New patients register in three fields and get a UHID straight away.
  • Check in for today's booking, or pick a department and doctor for a walk-in token.
  • English and Hindi. Resets after a minute of no use. Sign out on every screen.
  • The doctor presses Call next. The waiting-room TV shows only first name and last initial.

07 In-patient

A bed is filled only by an admission and freed only by a discharge.

Bed board

Every ward and bed by status: vacant, occupied, reserved, needs cleaning. Click a vacant bed to admit.

Admission

Patient search by name, UHID or phone; department, attending doctor, reason, provisional diagnosis, payer. No double admission.

Patient chart

Banner with UHID, IP number, bed, allergies, diet and critical alerts. Tabs for vitals, notes, medicines, tests, diet, billing, insurance, discharge.

Discharge

Final diagnosis, condition, medicines, advice, warning signs. The follow-up appointment is booked and the bed goes to housekeeping.

Patient summary

Plain language, printable, with one tap to send on WhatsApp or email.

Housekeeping

Ward attendants see beds to clean and mark them vacant. They cannot release an occupied bed.

08 Nursing

The next shift reads the record, not a colleague.

  • Vitals with automatic alerts: fever, tachycardia, low SpO₂, BP out of range. Impossible values are refused.
  • Nursing notes and doctor round notes on one timeline.
  • SBAR handover per patient. The incoming nurse acknowledges it, and nobody can acknowledge their own.
  • The ward handover sheet prints one card per patient with the latest vitals and pending tasks.
  • Medication chart: each dose is given, held or refused, with the person's name and the time.

09 Medicines

The prescription reaches the pharmacy before the patient does.

Sent→Being prepared→Ready for pickup→Collected
Safety check

Before it is saved

Allergy by drug family (penicillins, sulfa, NSAIDs), dangerous interactions and duplicates. The doctor must confirm to go ahead.

Quantity

Worked out for the pharmacist

Frequency × duration, including 1-0-1. TDS for 5 days gives 15 tablets.

Stock

Earliest expiry first

Batch and expiry on every receipt. Expired stock refused; low and expiring stock flagged.

Hand-over

Only to the right person

The pharmacist types the patient's UHID; a mismatch is refused. Substitutions need a reason.

Wards

In-patient orders

Doctor orders, ward indent goes to the pharmacy, issued medicines land on the running bill.

Patient

Sees the status

On their phone, so they walk to the counter when it is ready.

10 Diagnostics

No result leaves the lab until a second person checks it.

  • Doctors order from the chart or OPD: routine or urgent, with a clinical note.
  • The nurse marks the sample collected and a sample number S20261025-0014 is issued for the label.
  • Values are flagged against normal and critical ranges. Radiology and ECG take a written report.
  • A critical value raises a banner on the chart and on the doctor's and nurse's dashboards until someone acknowledges it.
  • Patients see their verified reports. In-patient tests are billed automatically.

11 Diet and meals

The doctor sets the diet, the patient chooses within it, and the kitchen gets a list.

  • Diets: normal, soft, liquid, diabetic, low salt, renal, high protein, nil by mouth.
  • Patients or nurses choose from dishes allowed for that diet, for today and the next two days.
  • The kitchen list shows every bed with diet, allergies, the dish to serve and a preparation count. Nil by mouth shows Do not serve.
  • The attendant marks the tray delivered, extras are billed, and the nurse records how much was eaten.

12 Revenue

The bill writes itself during the stay.

  • The price list (tariffs) has codes, categories, GST and active flags. A price change never alters an old bill.
  • Room charges add up each day from the bed type, alongside pharmacy, lab and meal extras.
  • Deposits, payments and refunds each get a receipt number. A refund can't exceed the money received.
  • A cancelled line needs a reason and stays visible, struck through.
  • The final bill freezes the stay, splits it between insurer and patient, and prints by category.

13 Insurance / TPA desk

Cashless stays without the email chase.

Payer at admissionInsurer, TPA, policy, sum insured, validity. Expired policies are refused.
Pre-authorisationBuilt from the admission. Each insurer decision is recorded with its amount and reference.
80% alertThe running bill nears the approved amount, so the desk raises an enhancement.
Discharge holdThe doctor can't discharge until final approval is in. The Discharge tab says why.
ClaimCLM-… filed after discharge with the bill and summary
SettlementUTR recorded. Every deduction must be explained line by line.

The desk's worklist shows what is waiting on insurers (with hours waited), stays without pre-auth, stays over 80%, discharges on hold, and claims by age. It supports PMJAY, CGHS, ECHS and corporate payers.

14 Trust

Every change is recorded with a name and a time.

  • Every create, update, delete and sign-in (including failed ones) is captured centrally in the database layer, so no screen can skip it.
  • The admin's Activity Log filters by record, action, date and person, and shows the values before and after.
  • Secrets are redacted. Patients see their own records only; staff notes are hidden from them.
  • One permission matrix drives pages, actions, menus and routing.
  • Signed HTTP-only cookies; the kiosk uses short-lived scoped tickets that can never act as a sign-in.

16 Under the hood

A modern web stack any Indian IT team can run.

ScreensNext.js 16 App Router and React 19. Server components; works in any browser, on phones, tablets and kiosks.
ActionsServer actions, one file per domain, each behind a permission guard. They return { success, message, data }.
Ruleslib/: dosing, drug safety, bill maths, lab flags, meal cut-offs, IST dates, identifiers. Pure functions, unit-tested.
PlatformA Prisma extension that audits every write and issues numbers: UHID, IP, RCP, BL, CLM, RX, LAB, sample.
DataPostgreSQL (local, Supabase or any managed host). Migrations carry the starter tariffs, formulary, tests and menu.
White-labelHospital name and UHID prefix come from settings, so any hospital, nursing home or institute can deploy it.

21 AI rollout

Start where the data and the risk are both manageable.

Now · 0–6 months

Documentation

Consultation scribe, drafted handover, drafted discharge summary, multilingual patient summaries.

Why first

A person reviews every output; it saves the most time; the data is already in SHDS.

Next · 6–12 months

Revenue and safety

ICD coding, claim-denial checks, NEWS2 early warning, lab delta checks and rule-based auto-verification of normal results.

Needs

NHCX access, a few months of the hospital's own claims and vitals.

Later · 12 months +

Prediction and imaging

Bed, staff and stock forecasting across the chain; deterioration models; radiology second reader once PACS is connected.

Needs

Multi-branch data, clinical validation, and the CDSCO approval path for medical-device AI.
